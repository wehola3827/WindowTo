❓ 常见问题解答

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

1. 为什么 WindowTo 需要“辅助功能”权限？ 1. Why does WindowTo require "Accessibility" permission?

由于 macOS 的安全沙盒限制，任何用于跨应用调整窗口大小和位置的系统工具，都必须在系统设置中获得“辅助功能（Accessibility）”授权。WindowTo 仅会利用此权限来实现窗口排版逻辑，绝不会以此收集、读取或传输您的任何个人屏幕或键盘输入信息。

Due to macOS security sandbox restrictions, any system utility designed to resize and reposition windows of other applications must be granted "Accessibility" authorization. WindowTo only utilizes this privilege to implement window layout actions, and will never collect, read, or transmit your screen contents or keystrokes.

2. 运行有什么系统版本限制？ 2. What are the system requirements?

WindowTo 深度适配了 macOS 的底层 API。为了获得最极致、流畅的窗口排版体验，我们建议您的系统运行在 macOS 12.0 或更高版本。

WindowTo is deeply optimized for native macOS APIs. To ensure the smoothest window management experience, we recommend using macOS 12.0 or later.

🔒 Privacy Policy

我们深知您的隐私至关重要。作为一款系统级辅助工具，WindowTo 的核心设计理念是完全本地化与零隐私接触：

We deeply value your privacy. As a system-level utility, WindowTo is engineered from the ground up to be **completely local and privacy-safe**:

  • 无数据收集： 应用绝不收集、不存储、不上报，也不会与任何第三方分享您的个人敏感数据。
  • Zero Data Collection: The app does not collect, store, report, or share any of your personal or sensitive data.
  • 全本地运行： 所有的窗口管理操作和计算均完全运行在您的设备本地，应用没有服务器端，也无需网络传输。
  • Processed Locally: All window management operations and layout computations are executed entirely on your local machine. No external servers or networking are required.
  • 无第三方插件： 应用未集成任何第三方统计、监测、行为分析或广告 SDK，从物理层杜绝潜在的隐私泄漏路径。
  • No Third-Party SDKs: This application contains no analytics, telemetry, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
