常见问题解答 (FAQ)

1. 为什么 WindowTo 需要“辅助功能”权限？
由于 macOS 的安全机制，任何用于调整其他应用程序窗口大小和位置的工具，都必须获得系统安全设置中的“辅助功能（Accessibility）”授权。WindowTo 仅利用该权限执行窗口调整操作，绝不会通过此权限收集或传输您的任何个人隐私。
2. 软件运行有什么系统版本要求？
WindowTo 推荐在 macOS 12.0 及以上版本运行，以便获得最佳的兼容性与流畅度。

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Why does WindowTo require "Accessibility" permission?
Due to macOS security restrictions, any utility designed to resize and reposition windows of other applications must be granted "Accessibility" authorization in system preferences. WindowTo only uses this permission to perform window adjustments and will never collect or transmit any of your personal data.
2. What are the system requirements?
WindowTo is recommended for macOS 12.0 or later to ensure the best performance and compatibility.
